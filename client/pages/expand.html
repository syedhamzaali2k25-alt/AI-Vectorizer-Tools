How it works

AI outpainting, explained

Extend a photo's canvas and let AI generate new content that matches the original seamlessly.

01

Upload your photo

Any PNG, JPG, or WebP — a portrait that's too tightly cropped, or a landscape you want wider.

02

Choose which edges to extend

Pick pixel amounts for left, right, up, and down — FLUX Fill Pro generates new content matching the existing lighting, colors, and style.

03

Download the expanded photo

Get back a larger canvas with a seamless extension — ready for a different aspect ratio or more breathing room in your composition.

Use cases

Who uses AI image expansion?

Social media & ads

Reformat a photo to fit a different aspect ratio — square, portrait, or widescreen — without cropping out the subject.

Product & lifestyle photos

Add more background around a tightly-framed product shot to give a listing or ad more visual breathing room.

Print & design layouts

Extend a photo to fill a print size or design template it wasn't originally shot for.
