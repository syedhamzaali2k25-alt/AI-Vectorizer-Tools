How it works

Bulk processing, explained

Processing images one at a time works fine when you only have one or two to edit. But if you're clearing backgrounds from an entire product catalog, upscaling a full photoshoot, or cleaning watermarks off a batch of licensed stock photos, uploading each image individually and waiting for it to finish before starting the next one adds up to a lot of wasted time. Bulk Processing is built specifically for that situation.

Start by choosing which tool you want to run — Background Remover, Image Upscaler, or Watermark Remover — from the dropdown at the top of this page. Each tool costs the same per image here as it does on its own dedicated page, so there's no extra charge for using the batch flow itself.

Once you've picked a tool, drop in up to 10 images at once, either by dragging them onto the upload area or clicking to browse your files. Every image can be up to 15MB, the same limit as the individual tool pages.

Before anything actually processes, you'll land on a review screen showing thumbnails of every image you selected. This is your chance to double check the batch — remove any image you added by mistake with the ✕ button, or click "Add more images" to bring in additional files without losing what you've already selected. Nothing is sent to our servers or charged against your credits until you actually click "Start."

Once you start, each image processes in sequence, and you'll see a live status badge on every thumbnail update in real time — processing, completed, or failed. If one image in the batch fails for any reason, the rest continue normally; a single failure never stops the batch or affects your other images, and you're never charged credits for the one that failed.

When everything finishes, a "Download all as ZIP" button becomes available. Click it once, and every successfully processed image is bundled into a single ZIP file — no need to click through and download each result one at a time.

Use cases

Who uses bulk processing?

E-commerce sellers and catalog managers often have dozens or hundreds of product photos that all need the same background removed before they can go live on a marketplace listing. Instead of uploading each photo separately and waiting for it to finish, a whole batch can be queued up and left to process while you work on something else, then downloaded as one ZIP ready to import back into your store.

Photographers and creative agencies frequently need to upscale an entire shoot's worth of images at once — for print delivery, a client handoff, or preparing assets for a larger display. Running that shoot through Bulk Processing means the whole set comes back at higher resolution together, rather than one photo at a time.

Content, marketing, and social media teams working with licensed stock photography sometimes need to clear preview watermarks from a whole batch of images before dropping them into campaigns, decks, or a content calendar. Bulk Processing handles that cleanup across the entire set in one pass instead of requiring a separate visit to the Watermark Remover tool for every single image.
