PNG vs SVG: What's the Difference?

A PNG stores your image as a grid of pixels. It's great for the web at a fixed size, but enlarge it, and it blurs. An SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) stores the image as mathematical paths, so it scales to any size, from a favicon to a billboard with perfectly sharp edges.

That's why designers, printers, and cutting machines want SVG.
